# CPIT Signage Customer Handoff Guide

## What the System Does

The CPIT Signage player displays a repeating sequence of images and created message signs on the lobby television.

Authorized users can:

- Upload pictures
- Create informational signs
- Change display duration
- Change display order
- Temporarily disable content
- Delete outdated content

## Normal Operation

The player should start automatically after power is restored.

No keyboard or mouse is required for normal television playback.

## Creating a Sign

1. Open the CPIT Signage administration page.
2. Enter a title, message, or both.
3. Optionally add a footer.
4. Choose colors and alignment.
5. Set the display duration.
6. Review the live preview.
7. Select **Create Sign**.

The system generates a full-screen image and adds it to the playlist.

## Uploading Pictures

1. Use the Upload Images section.
2. Drag pictures into the upload area, or click to browse.
3. Wait for confirmation.
4. The page refreshes and the new content appears in the playlist.

## Reordering Content

Use the move handle beside an item to drag it into the desired position.

The television should reflect the new order within approximately 15 seconds.

## Disabling Content

Uncheck the Enabled box and save the playlist.

This keeps the content stored but removes it from playback.

## Deleting Content

Select Delete beside an item and confirm.

Deletion permanently removes both the playlist record and the media file.

## Important Notes

- Do not unplug the player while content is being uploaded.
- Do not expose the administration page directly to the public Internet.
- Contact support if the display remains blank after a power cycle.
